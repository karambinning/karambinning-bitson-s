What we do

A proper fish and chip shop, done the long way round.

There's a takeaway counter on the street and a cafe up the stairs. Cod, haddock, chips, mushy peas, a pie if you fancy one, scallops if you know to ask. The batter is the bit everyone writes home about.

We've been on Blackfriars Street for over four decades. The menu hasn't changed much. The fryer is on by eleven, off by three or seven or eight depending on the day. We take cash. We don't take ourselves seriously.

Three things to know

Walk in. Sit down. Or take it away.

No. 1

The counter.

Open the front door, you're at the fryer. Order, pay (cash), wrapped to go. Most of King's Lynn knows the drill — it's the queue out the door at lunch.

No. 2

The cafe upstairs.

A flight of stairs, a handful of tables, a proper sit-down feed. Same kitchen, same batter, served on a plate with a cup of tea instead of out of paper.

No. 3

Cash only.

No card machines. No app, no QR, no contactless. There's a cashpoint a short walk away on the High Street if you've come empty-handed.
