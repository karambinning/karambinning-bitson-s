What we do

A proper fish and chip shop, done the long way round.

There's a takeaway counter on the street and a cafe up the stairs. Cod and haddock, chips, and the things you'd expect from a King's Lynn chip shop. The batter is the bit people write home about — that's the beef dripping.

We've been on Blackfriars Street for over four decades. The menu hasn't changed much. The fryer is on for lunch, and longer some days — ring the shop if you're coming from a way off.

Three things to know

Walk in. Sit down. Or take it away.

No. 1

The counter.

Open the front door, you're at the fryer. Order, pay (cash), wrapped to go. Most of King's Lynn knows the drill — it's the queue out the door at lunch.

No. 2

The cafe upstairs.

A flight of stairs, a handful of tables, a proper sit-down feed. Same kitchen, same batter, served on a plate with a cup of tea instead of out of paper.

No. 3

Cooked in beef dripping.

The bit you can't fake. It's why the batter shatters when you bite it and why the chips don't go soft on the walk back to the river. Same way it's been done here since the eighties.
