About the shop

A chip shop, plain
and proper.

Bitson's has been on Blackfriars Street for over four decades. The fryer is fired up on beef dripping. The chips go out in paper. That's mostly it.

The shop

Forty-six years on the same corner.

Bitson's sits at number six Blackfriars Street, a few minutes' walk from the river. The takeaway window opens straight onto the street. A narrow staircase runs up to the cafe.

Two routes to the same food. Wrapped in paper and eaten walking back through Tuesday Market. Or plated up, served sitting down in the room above. Either way, the fish comes out of the same fryer, the chips off the same range, all of it cooked in beef dripping — the way it's been done here for over four decades.

That's the thing that shows up in the batter — the crisp, the colour, the not-too-thick, not-too-thin of it. It's the unfussy part of King's Lynn that doesn't change.

The food

Cod. Haddock. Chips.

The menu is the kind of menu a chip shop has when it's been a chip shop for forty-six years. Nothing fancy. Nothing on a slate.

Cod and haddock are the headliners — fresh, battered to a colour you'd call ‘chippy gold’, fried in beef dripping, served with chips that have been through the range twice. Salt and vinegar from the actual bottles.

It is a working chip shop, not a heritage display. That's why it's still here after forty-six years.

The things that don't change

Three reasons, unchanged.

No. 1

Beef dripping.

The fryer is fired up on beef dripping — not vegetable oil. It's why the batter goes that proper deep gold and shatters when you bite into it. The bit you can't fake with a thermostat.

No. 2

Forty-six years here.

The shop has been on Blackfriars Street since long before the lane went one-way. Generations of Lynn folk have queued at this counter; some now bring their grandchildren in for the upstairs cafe.

No. 3

The cafe upstairs.

Same food, plated. A few tables, a few chairs, a window onto the street. If you want to sit down, that's where to go — the queue downstairs is for paper-wrapped takeaway.
