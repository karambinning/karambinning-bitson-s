Menu · Counter & Cafe

The menu.
What we serve.

Fresh fish in proper batter, chips off the range, a few sides and a pie or two. Prices on the chalkboard in the shop — ring the counter on 01553 774760 if you want to know what today's catch is doing.

From the Fryer The headliners

Cod & Chips

Fresh cod in the house batter — crisp, gold, never claggy. Served with a generous portion of chips off the range.

Haddock & Chips

A slightly sweeter, flakier white fish for the haddock devotees. Same batter, same chips, same paper wrap.

Fish Cake & Chips

House fish cakes, battered. A regulars' favourite — ask at the counter.

Sides & Extras The supporting cast

Chips

Twice through the range. Salted at the counter, vinegar from the bottle if you'd like.

Potato Scallops

Slices of potato dipped in the same batter — the not-on-every-menu item that keeps the connoisseurs coming back. Ask if you can't see them in the warmer.

Mushy Peas

Bright, slightly squashy, the way they should be.

Curry Sauce · Gravy

Poured over chips, or in a pot to dip into. Both available.

Pies & Pasties For when you don't fancy fish

Steak Pie

A proper one. With chips, or on its own.

Chicken & Mushroom Pie

Hot crust, hot filling. Eat the lid first.

Sausage in Batter · Saveloy

The chip-shop classics. Always available, always cheap.

Upstairs in the Cafe For sitting down

Sit-down Fish & Chips

Same kitchen as the counter, served on a plate at a table with cutlery and the proper bread and butter.

Pot of Tea

Strong, brown, in a metal pot. The only acceptable accompaniment to a sit-down plate of chips.

Soft Drinks

Cans from the fridge.

Cash only · Always has been

No card machine, no contactless, no app. There's a cashpoint on the High Street, two minutes' walk. Bring notes for the cafe upstairs.
