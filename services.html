The menu.
What we serve.

Fresh fish in proper batter, cooked in beef dripping, served with chips off the range. Prices on the board in the shop — ring the counter on 01553 774760 for today's specifics, or pop in and see what's on.

From the Fryer The headliners

Cod & Chips

Fresh cod in the house batter — fried in beef dripping until it's that proper deep gold. Served with a generous portion of chips off the range.

Haddock & Chips

A slightly sweeter, flakier white fish for the haddock devotees. Same batter, same beef dripping, same paper wrap.

Just Chips

Twice through the range. Salted at the counter, vinegar from the bottle if you'd like.

Upstairs in the Cafe For sitting down

Sit-down Fish & Chips

Same kitchen as the counter, served on a plate at a table with cutlery instead of out of paper.

Pot of Tea

The only acceptable accompaniment to a sit-down plate of chips.

The rest is on the board

Sides, sauces, daily extras — whatever's on goes on the chalkboard in the shop. Ring 01553 774760 if you want to know what we've got today.
